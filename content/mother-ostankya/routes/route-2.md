## Opening

**Prepare an expedition rather than flee the starting war**

Make the southern journey possible without abandoning an unresolved homeland.

This is a collecting expedition, not a conquest of the continent. The archive frames Route II as Toil & Trouble: a hunting caravan of Akshina guides, a persistent Warrior escort, a Beasts Hag, spiders and heavy forest animals, led south from Naggaroth to collect the three named ingredients. The route identity in its own words: choose manageable candidate settlements rather than conquering all eighteen sites automatically, hold the collection bases the objective actually requires, treat “search” as an unverified action rather than a documented hero action, and build the Devotion and recovery infrastructure that keeps a long journey viable. The Early muster (The wandering gatherers) is the expedition's identity, not a turn-one roster (Armies: Early).

- **Remain in Naggaroth.** The three ingredient groups are in Lustria on Immortal Empires. Relocating to Kislev adds a return journey without changing those targets.
- **Secure the home approach and scout south.** Finish the starting war, examine Morathi's position and remove a direct threat that blocks your expedition when feasible. You do not need to finish the entire Route III target list before departing.
- **Verify the first ingredient rule.** Read all three objectives before planning land purchases or wars. The ledger defaults to the archived three controlled sites per group, but the current public guide omits that quota. Test the first action and record the result.
- **Develop a recruiting capital and a home response.** Silent Grove and affordable animal recruitment support the caravan. Leave an appropriate defence instead of taking every useful unit away and relying on diplomacy you have not established (Settlements & economy: A forest recruitment capital; Armies: The village wardens).

The starting war is resolved, the homeland can survive your absence, and the first collecting cluster and its live rules are identified.

## Early → Mid

**Establish the northern collecting stations**

Take a useful set of locations and make them genuinely support the journey.

- **Compare the Itxi Grub candidates.** Skeggi, Hexoatl, Pahuax, Floating Pyramid, Spektazuma and The Blood Hall are alternatives in the northern cluster. Current ownership and access decide the practical order, not the printed order. Prefer a manageable set of current owners and connected bases; do not declare war on a powerful Lizardman faction simply because its capital appears in the list.
- **Fight one regional war at a time.** Do not attack several powerful Lizardman factions simultaneously to force a perfectly neat route. A connected set of easier holdings can be worth more than a spectacular capital.
- **Occupy, verify and retain.** Check objective credit after each relevant capture or other accepted action. Under the archived rules, direct control matters; mark credited holdings, not merely places you visited. Do not invent a hero “search” action — published “search” wording is not a documented hero action (Settlements & economy: A Lustrian collecting station).
- **Make a real field station.** Build Hallowed recovery in an actual turn-ending region. Stabilise Devotion and income. Prepare for climate penalties rather than assuming a forest-themed faction treats every jungle as suitable.

The first ingredient group has the required live credit and a station exists from which the army can recover and continue.

## Mid → Late

**Cross the jungle without unravelling behind you**

Secure the central and southern groups through a sustained expedition.

- **Choose the Jungle Lotus line.** Tlaxtlan, Xlanhuapec, The Sacred Pools, The Awakening, The Star Tower and Altar of the Horned Rat are the central/eastern candidates. Choose a central-to-coastal line that can be supplied; an eastern coastal station may support the next leg, while a distant isolated conquest may not. Hostile pirate or other non-allied holdings may provide alternatives to attacking several Lizardman powers at once; current ownership decides.
- **Preserve climate protection.** Aim to retain 75+ Devotion in unsuitable-climate holdings where possible. Spending 50 on an invocation can undo it — a province at 90 falls to 40. Use the Devotion discipline before trading long-term stability for a brief army bonus.
- **Prepare a large-threat answer.** Move into the Late or Great hunt template according to actual opponents. Incarnates and anti-large reserves supplement the escort; Akshina and Things retain their infantry-hunting roles (Armies: Late, Great hunt, Home guard).
- **Stage the Coatl expedition.** Scout Hualotal, Great Turtle Isle, The Golden Colossus, Mine of the Bearded Skulls, Oyxl and Kaiax. Compare island versus mainland access, and leave enough defence at earlier credited stations before the last expedition.

**Retain the stations and move the caravan safely**

Hold credited holdings, protect the line of march and keep the Hex, Incantation and Devotion support working while the host travels the jungle.

- **Retain the credited stations.** Ingredient control is different from a remembered visit or a crafted trinket. Hold credited locations until the route is awarded; do not sell a required station because you visited it once.
- **Protect the route between credited sites.** Use an escort army or a protected border. A distant ally is not assumed to satisfy an ownership requirement (Settlements & economy: A Lustrian collecting station).
- **Use Purification on the staging route.** Cure plagues and remove relevant corruption before they compromise an operation; manage Devotion separately.
- **Use Jinxed Land only to a useful destination.** A forest shortcut still needs safe arrival and onward access — scout the arrival area and the remaining journey before moving the caravan.
- **Protect collecting stations at 75+ Devotion.** Do not spend away the climate protection without a good reason. Build Devotion and recovery where the operation actually needs it (Workshop: Devotion).
- **Apply protective Incantations before battle.** Craft for the next matchup, confirm the intended recipient in the prebattle interface, and keep one emergency package instead of hoarding Essence (Workshop: Incantations).

The remaining sites are scouted, your earlier holdings remain credited, and the expedition has money, replacement access and a secure next stopping point.

## Victory push

**Close the final quota, not an entire continent**

Verify ownership and the award before dismantling the expedition's support network.

- **Audit all three groups together.** Count currently credited locations in each group. A historical capture is not the same as present control. Update the provisional quotas to match the installed VCO panel.
- **Choose the least disruptive missing site.** Use the candidate alternatives to avoid an unnecessary new major war. A legal settlement trade may work under direct-control rules, but verify that your actual action credits rather than assuming it does.
- **Retain the stations through the trigger.** Under the archived code, the three group requirements are satisfied together. A lost first site can undo the advantage of taking the last one; do not give away the first group's towns just because that leg finished many turns ago. Confirm the award before selling collecting stations or breaking a relationship used for a legitimate credit.
- **Confirm the custom reward and route victory.** Once the route is awarded, equip and inspect the item; reward receipt is checked separately from the victory notification. VCO's changelog records a missing-victory-rewards fix dated 27 September that is not retroactive — load a save from before the trigger to benefit. Decide whether to return, hold Lustria or pivot north before transferring bases; the objective does not require owning all eighteen listed candidates.

## Territory policy

Hold the needed collecting stations and a coherent supporting corridor. A string of remote fragile towns is not efficient merely because the total territory is small. Keep or transfer other conquests according to security and the actual control requirement.

- **Stations are what this route holds territory for.** A Lustrian field station is a required-site holding with a clear retention job: occupied, credited and defended, with Hallowed recovery and sufficient Devotion for the local climate — not a disposable raid (Settlements & economy: A Lustrian collecting station).
- **The corridor supports the caravan.** Recovery stations where the army actually ends turns, a defended route between credited sites and a forward recruiting branch where distance justifies it keep the expedition viable (Settlements & economy: A regional recovery station, A gain with an exit plan).
- **The homeland still funds the expedition.** The forest recruitment capital and ordinary income keep the army replaced while the caravan is away; the Hut's passive Essence and stable provincial Devotion finance the journey (Settlements & economy: A forest recruitment capital, The landmark's home).

## Diplomacy

Scout current owners before committing. Prefer one manageable enemy at a time; use legal settlement transactions or a friendly corridor where possible. Do not assume that a military ally's ownership counts for this route's ingredient objectives.

- **Read the owner board before each commitment.** The printed candidate order is not an attack order. Current ownership and access decide which sites on the group's list are worth taking now.
- **Keep the war list short.** One manageable enemy at a time. A nearby required target list belongs to Route III, not to this route's opening war plan — remove a direct threat that blocks the expedition when feasible without clearing the whole list.
- **A friendly corridor is an option, not a promise.** Legal settlement transactions may shorten the campaign, but a transfer or trade credits only while the live rule accepts it. Treat the route as direct control until the live objective says otherwise.

## Transition → route-1

Keep the experienced coven and the income from the expedition. Review the five Hex unlocks already earned through the journey, then focus on the remaining spending and quest stages. There is no need to sail home to complete the location-independent ritual objective, though a secure base and full army health remain useful. Keep the Hut, veteran casters and forest recruitment; leave a home response force while the ritual work proceeds.

## Transition → route-3

Finish and confirm the ingredient route before handing away required towns. Keep the best Lustrian income and a small defensive force; send the main expedition north, or establish a second recruiting army in the old homeland. Audit the six New World enemies and existing settlement-action credit. The expedition becomes a two-theatre campaign, not a reason to throw away its economy.
