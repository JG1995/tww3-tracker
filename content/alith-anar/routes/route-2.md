## Opening

**Secure a base for a deliberate expedition**

Start at The Monoliths and prepare the search before leaving a live war behind.

This is the most exploratory route in the guide: a moonlit recovery expedition built around a travelling company and well-chosen bases rather than an automatic conquest of the entire continent. Follow the rumour, not every border.

- **Read the current search wording first.** Open Route II and compare the eight candidates with the atlas; record any displayed quota, search interaction or special instruction. The historical seven-of-eight quota is only a planning default until verified.
- **Win the starting local conflict.** Use the same practical spear/bow foundation, add an Aesanar Camp at tier two and retain the useful starting specialists. Security and a replenishment base come before a long southward excursion.
- **Identify candidate owners early.** The eight search targets are The Twisted Glade and Hag Hall in the northern search area; Clarak Spire and Plain of Dogs as another practical group; The Witchwood as a diplomatic question; and Arnheim, the Ancient City of Quintex and Tyrant Peak in the south. Current borders and army locations decide the order, not a promise of perfectly adjacent stops.
- **Budget the journey.** Leave enough income and a modest defender to keep the home base safe. Do not buy a Merwyrm or two Skycutters before you can afford an ordinary escort and caster.
- **Keep the expedition’s identity honest.** Do not assume every candidate must be conquered or that a single random capture is guaranteed to reveal the amulet, and do not give Alith Aislinn’s Dragonships just because the escort has naval units.

## Early → Mid

**Make the first qualifying searches**

Prove how the mission credits a location before planning the whole campaign around an assumption.

- **Test one inexpensive interaction.** Use an available conquest or eligible diplomatic control opportunity and compare the mission before and after. Mark the actual credited result, not merely a treaty or a capture notification.
- **Keep the army a travelling company.** Walkers remain the main hunters; use a shielded centre and one Mage. Sea Guard become useful as the economy develops, and a Mist Mage is a thematic replacement when a Harbour and capacity make one available; do not dismiss a developed Shadows Mage purely to match the roster.
- **Do not turn every rumour into total war.** A candidate held by a powerful neutral faction might be the one you skip if the live quota permits. A useful ally can be better than another coastline you must defend.
- **Retain credit while uncertain.** Until the active mission makes clear that a search is permanently recorded, keep qualifying control or the relevant agreement intact. Do not immediately transfer the site away.
- **Keep the agent programme optional.** A detached Noble can gather intelligence and take opportunistic actions, but the agent budget must not replace the main military objective.

The economy feeds the expedition in order: the war hub first, then a port or recovery base, then limited search-site investment. Only promote a second capital to specialist production when the distance genuinely justifies it.

## Mid → Late

**Establish the middle staging line**

Search efficiently without letting a travel-heavy route empty the treasury.

- **Work through logical groups.** Investigate Clarak Spire and Plain of Dogs where practical, then the Witchwood relationship and the southern Arnheim/Quintex/Tyrant Peak area. These are planning groups, not a claim about the hidden amulet location.
- **Treat the Witchwood as diplomacy first.** A friendly Sisters of Twilight faction can cover territory and provide a useful partner. Check whether a military alliance gives mission credit; non-aggression or trade alone should not be entered as equivalent.
- **Keep a real recovery station.** A secure port or developed candidate city with growth/replenishment and ordinary replacements prevents a damaged expedition from facing a long retreat. Expensive new recruitment should solve a repeated logistical problem, not prove status.
- **Use the court sparingly but purposefully.** Reserve Influence for a needed alliance/character and a useful patronage. Pathways, target intelligence and a reserved political budget matter most; use a mark that advances the current operation instead of abandoning the route for a remote bounty. A small escort flight, for example two Skycutters, is an optional thematic project after the army’s core is paid for.

**Resolve the southern candidates**

Prepare for the actual military opposition without losing sight of the search.

- **Choose the Morathi problem consciously.** The Ancient City of Quintex can be a substantial operation if Morathi is strong. Scout her armies and corruption, then concentrate force or choose another qualifying candidate if the current rules allow it. Do not promise it is a free final stop.
- **Complete Arnheim and Tyrant Peak as required.** Check current owners and pathing. Finish the missing credits rather than occupying all intervening land merely to make the realm look continuous.
- **Keep magic and armour penetration credible.** Dark Elf armour and missiles punish an all-light bow army. Retain the screen, bolts and anti-armour reserve even in the moonlit themed roster.

## Victory push

**Recover the amulet, then verify the award**

Follow the current mission’s finish instead of inventing a treasure roll.

- **Obey the final live instruction.** Complete the displayed search/control quota or any follow-up explicitly issued by the mod. The public author guide does not establish a guaranteed random location or a mandatory eight-city conquest; do not invent an extra quest if none is issued.
- **Keep the right settlements qualifying.** Maintain the required control until the award processes. A manually filled meter or planning record is not the game’s victory trigger.
- **Confirm the item and victory separately.** Record the Amulet of Sunfire when it is actually received and the VCO route award when the game displays it. A generic magic-item drop is not the same event and not proof that the route reward granted.
- **Choose what the expedition becomes.** You can finish here, turn north towards full reconquest, or return to Ulthuan and build the Eternal War network. Keep the useful port and the veteran company either way.

## Territory policy

Keep the original security base and the productive search/staging territory the mission needs. Retain qualifying control until victory is confirmed and use diplomatic credit only where the actual panel accepts it; do not transfer a credited site away while the search is still recorded. Build the economy in the route’s order — war hub, port or recovery base, then limited search-site investment — and promote a second capital to specialist production only when the distance justifies it. Occupying all intervening land merely to make the realm look continuous is not the objective.

## Diplomacy

Preserve a friendly Witchwood where possible. Seek an eligible military alliance — or choose a different candidate if the live quota allows. Trade and non-aggression alone are not proof of search credit. Audit agreements and recheck earlier credits before the final push: a broken military alliance or a recaptured site can matter if the objective checks present control.

## Transition → route-1

Keep the safe port and the useful searches, then turn the expedition towards the six control cities. Add a holding army and a heavier escort through ordinary recruitment; the walkers and Mage remain valuable. Your southern gains are support assets, not a reason to conquer every neighbour first.

## Transition → route-3

The relic expedition’s scouting, ports and diplomatic contacts are useful for the wider hunt. Secure Tor Anlec and begin its growth, recruit dedicated Noble agents and inspect the wounded-lord indicators. Keep the travelling company; move its specialist choices towards the next actual target rather than rebuilding everything.
