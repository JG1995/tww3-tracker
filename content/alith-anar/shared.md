## Fight for the preview, not the label

::claim inferred src=smart,flc
These are balanced, thematic recommendations for Normal / Normal with Smart Autoresolve, not a measured matchup formula or benchmark. Check the casualty estimate as well as the verdict: replacing an expensive veteran can make a nominal victory a poor trade. A stalking attack can improve the campaign engagement, but it is not a guarantee and no numerical Smart Autoresolve bonus is established here. If the force would be too depleted to survive retaliation, recover before the next battle or reinforce it rather than treating the verdict as safe.
::

- Keep the Shadow-walker identity without making every force fragile. Group the walkers into two nearby firing sections instead of sending each unit to a different flank; a conventional escort makes a failed ambush easier to manage.
- Do not walk unsupported, lightly armoured hunters into a prolonged frontal exchange. Bows and poison are not universal armour-piercing answers. Keep a spear screen, clear firing lanes, and retain Eagle Claws or a suitable melee reserve when armour or cavalry demands it. Add support or replace a small number of vulnerable slots before abandoning the faction's theme for an all-archer or all-monster force.
- After a difficult clash, rebuild a screen, two bow sections, and one reserve before pressing on. Pause, reposition, or take a recovery turn when that prevents avoidable veteran losses.
- Use Alith's decoy, rear shots, slowing effects, and target selection as manual-battle tools. Do not assume they have equivalent value in the autoresolve calculation. When there is no ambush, deploy conventionally and secure a safe lane or breach rather than feeding every unit into a narrow siege approach.
- The recommendations assume Normal / Normal. A higher difficulty is optional; reassess treasury room, replenishment, and predicted casualties instead of treating the theme as a handicap to overcome.

## Equip the role before the rarity

These are priorities, not an automatic equipment assignment. Match useful items to the character's job before chasing a perfect loadout.

::claim inferred src=flc,vco
The archive's equipment roles are strategic recommendations, not observed equipment assignments or a claim that every item is available. Check the current character inventory and treat the Amulet of Sunfire as a Route II reward only when the live game actually awards it.
::

- **Alith:** favour ranged output, personal speed, missile defence, and useful bound tools. As a foot character, he should not be left exposed or trapped just to improve damage.
- **Embedded Noble:** favour armour, ward protection, and a healing consumable so the bodyguard remains useful in a prolonged fight.
- **Mage:** reserve arcane equipment for the caster who will spend the Winds of Magic. Avoid duplicate, low-impact activations that add more clicks than value.
- **Detached Noble:** prioritise campaign movement and relevant action-success support. Melee equipment does not make a low-success assassination a sound gamble.
- **Supporting commander:** keep the loadout aligned with the chosen role; a foot bow Princess and a mounted combat Prince need different support.
- **Signature rewards:** the Amulet of Sunfire is described as a Route II campaign reward, not a purchasable generic item or a prerequisite for every army. Do not spend as if a post-victory reward were already available.

The game, not this guide, assigns items. Use its unequipped-items view and current character details. A notable artefact on Alith and modest survivability for companions are enough; every slot does not need a perfect item.

## Build the army you can recruit

::claim historical src=flc,tides
The archive assumes all WH3 DLC and WH1/WH2 free content, and distinguishes Alith's free Shadow-walkers from the separately paid Queen & Crone Shadow Warriors. It treats WH3 naval units as optional and does not require Sisters of Avelorn, Handmaidens, Warden & Paunch units, or another lord's exclusive campaign system. The linked annotations were retained from the archive and were not independently rechecked in this package; confirm current recruitment access and ownership in the live game before relying on any unit.
::

- Keep Shadow-walkers distinct from ordinary Shadow Warriors; the archive identifies the former as Alith's free faction unit and the latter as a paid unit.
- A base High Elf escort can supply the ordinary line, bows, Sea Guard, White Lions infantry, Swordmasters, Phoenix Guard, Eagle Claws, cavalry, and dragons. White Lions infantry are not the paid War Lions monster unit.
- Mist Mages, Ship's Company, Oceanids, Merwyrms, and Skycutters are optional WH3 additions in the archive's setup, not requirements for the core company.
- Do not plan around Sisters of Avelorn, Handmaidens, Archmages, Silverin Guard, Rangers, War Lions, Lion Chariots, or Arcane Phoenixes as hidden prerequisites. The archive treats Aislinn's Dragonships, Sea Patrol Outposts, and Sea Lanes as distinct from Alith's faction mechanics.
- A Shadows Mage is the archive's default scout-army partner. Life magic can support a monster-heavy force, Light can restrain enemies, and a Mist Mage can support a coastal expedition; these are optional role choices, not universal unlock requirements.
- Do not rely on confederating Alarielle, Eltharion, or another paid WH2 character. DLC ownership, available characters, and any route-specific diplomatic credit are separate questions; check the actual roster and live objective rather than assuming one proves the other.

## Keep the next war affordable

::claim verify-in-campaign src=build-info,industry,order,camp,workshop,archive,court2,anlec
The archive's budgets and building gates are estimates based on annotated reference data, including a Patch 9.0 data lead; this package has not verified the player's installed patch, modifiers, building tiers, costs, or upkeep. Before recruiting or upgrading, inspect the current treasury, net income after existing upkeep, the proposed force's actual added upkeep, immediate recruitment/construction cost, and the amount you need to leave available for losses and recovery. Check the live building tier and price before using an archived gate. Do not fund permanent upkeep with hypothetical sacking, loot, or a reward that arrives only after victory. If the recurring budget or reserve is inadequate, delay the purchase, recruit in smaller batches, or recover before opening another front.
::

- **Build in useful batches.** Keep the effective first army; add Shadow-walkers gradually while ordinary archers and spears still do useful work. Retain cash for casualties and the next settlement investment instead of replacing every unit at once.
- **Give a capital the specialist work.** The archive's first production spine uses Aesanar Camp, Elven Workshop, and Archive; distribute supporting income or growth buildings among nearby settlements when slots tighten. Do not duplicate every recruitment branch in every town.
- **Treat slots and tiers as constraints.** Main chains, resources, and ports all compete for settlement space. Spread broadly useful growth and income through a province; construct capital-only chains only where an actual capital is available. The archive lists Aesanar Camp and Elven Workshop at tier two, Archive and ordinary Harbour at tier three, Elven Court at tier four, and the Black Citadel of Anlec landmark at tier five; verify the current building panel and costs before planning around those archived values.
- **Price Control and prosperity together.** The archive recommends comparing the province's actual extra income from Control and prosperity with the cost of the next tier. Treat conditional income as a live province value, not a guaranteed return.
- **Compare an upgrade with the campaign horizon.** Use the actual net extra income after modifiers and the time until the building operates. The archive's simple payoff estimate ignored changing Control, growth, and compounding. A port that enables trade or a needed recruitment gate can justify an upgrade that looks slow on income alone; a good-looking payoff can still wait when a holding is undefended or an army is depleted.
- **Protect recurring income.** Do not count one-off sacking or future battle loot as reliable upkeep. Add another army only after checking its actual Supply Lines and upkeep modifiers and the treasury's ability to cover recruitment, construction, losses, and the next enemy turn; there is no fixed cost per twenty-unit army.
- **Invest where distance makes it useful.** A wider route may justify a second production or recovery hub when the time and cost of returning home exceed the investment. Do not build a second hub just because a route is long on the map.
- **Use diplomacy to reduce fronts.** Trade, military access, and carefully chosen alliances may protect the budget better than an expensive army watching a friendly border. They do not, by themselves, prove that a VCO control objective or a construction-access rule is satisfied.
- **Do not impose a universal province cap.** Ambushes do not make a broad economy unnecessary. Hold territory when it supplies, protects, or enables the selected route's objectives; the later route plans own the specific retention policy.

Influence is a separate budget from treasury and Favour of the Phoenix King. The archive's script advises using confirmed passive Influence income, retaining a character / diplomacy reserve, and avoiding speculative mission rewards; do not treat gold or Favour as spendable Influence or empty the wallet for an unneeded seat. Route-specific court and mechanic priorities belong with the later mechanics and route references. The archive's settlement ledger called each town's role and next purchase a planning label, not a game mechanic; the current app has no saved holdings editor, so use the route and settlement references rather than expecting a recorded label to change the campaign.

## A short end-of-turn routine

Research is the faction's live game queue; changing the guide route changes the displayed recommendation, not completed research, and this app does not save technology checkmarks.

::claim inferred src=tech,tech-links
When a research gate is unavailable, the archive recommends choosing another useful unlocked technology and pursuing a prerequisite only when its recruitment or economy role is worthwhile, rather than starting an unnecessary war for a small bonus. The linked technology annotations are archival and were not independently rechecked for this package.
::

1. **Check the army's next position.** Could it survive retaliation if an ambush fails or the next autoresolve costs more than expected? Recover, recruit a practical replacement, or change the approach before committing again.
2. **Check the next building and unlock.** Does construction serve this route rather than duplicate an unused chain? Choose the next useful available skill or research priority and follow the actual gate; do not invent skill points or prerequisites.
3. **Check time-sensitive work.** Review any live target deadline, rite, or recruitment wait that matters to the selected route. The archive says the Hand is summoned near Alith, so check the current game display and Alith's location before relying on it for a distant target; do not assume it appears at the original capital.
4. **Check each resource separately.** Keep working reserves for treasury, Influence, and Favour; they are not interchangeable. Avoid committing future rewards or unconfirmed income.
5. **Check the one condition this operation was meant to advance.** Compare the live objective or counter before and after the relevant action. A planning tick or an atlas note is not proof of in-game credit.

::claim verify-in-campaign src=rite-ui,hand
The archive's cited 9.0 UI note says the Hand is summoned near Alith. This package did not verify the player's current game or mod, or the live summon preview. Check the displayed summon location before committing the one-use agent to a distant target; if its current position or travel makes the plan unsafe, move Alith into reach first or use another available agent or army rather than assuming a remote spawn.
::
