## Roster access and the common opening

Build from the army and territory you actually have. Clear the immediate threat before opening several new fronts, retain useful starting troops, and replace them by role as recruitment and budget allow. The later templates are goals, not turn-one shopping lists.

The archive’s common army foundation is Cathay’s Jade infantry, crossbows, and cannon support. Treat newer or specialist choices—including Celestial Generals, Gate Masters, lions, War Drums, and Tiger troops—as optional until your live recruitment and capacity screens confirm access; Tiger recruitment does not imply Bhashiva’s separate campaign systems are active. Allied or mercenary Ogres likewise depend on an available pool, capacity, and diplomacy. Use an available troop in a missing hero slot rather than delaying a workable force, and do not copy a unique hero into several armies.

The intended ownership assumption is all WH3 DLC and only free WH1/WH2 content; do not make a paid older-game character a prerequisite. Preserve a useful starting elite instead of disbanding it to match a later roster. This is Zhao Ming’s guide: it does not require Bhashiva’s Iron Favour or Goals, Yuan Bo’s Steel and Stone or astromantic relays, another faction’s special armies, or a borrowed teleport system. The archive’s access notes cite [CA’s Cathay 8.0 rework](https://community.creative-assembly.com/total-war/total-war-warhammer/blogs/98-total-war-warhammer-iii-update-8-0-patch-notes) and [Bhashiva’s faction overview](https://community.creative-assembly.com/total-war/total-war-warhammer/blogs/97-total-war-warhammer-iii-bhashiva-character-pack-what-s-included); those source records remain archive-qualified, not independently rechecked here.

## Smart Autoresolve • preserve the company

These are Normal/Normal combined-arms recommendations, not a tested numerical optimum for an identified Smart Autoresolve version. Check the predicted unit losses, not only the victory label. Losing a veteran or a convoy’s only answer to a large target can make a nominal win a poor campaign trade.

- Retain a durable line, damage that can handle armour and large targets, ranged support, and a reserve. That core also remains usable in manual battles.
- Match the visible threat: keep an anti-large answer for cavalry and monsters, focus fire on dangerous targets, and do not expect unsupported Peasant bows to solve armour.
- Recover before a damaging follow-up. A turn spent replenishing can protect a rare unit and avoid compounding casualties; do not force-march a depleted army into another threat.
- Do not assume the mod values active tricks as the manual battle does. Battle Harmony positioning, spells, Zhao’s dragon intervention, and consumable buffs have no verified Smart Autoresolve valuation here.
- Fight a consequential quest, convoy ambush, or close field battle manually when the forecast risks the force or operation. Pause to restore the line, protect the backline, and stop unnecessary pursuit.

For a low-micro manual fallback, group the army around three jobs: a shielded line, nearby ranged units, and one reserve or flanking group. Keep shooters near their protectors, leave clear lanes for guns and artillery, place artillery and support behind a protected frontage, cover a threatened flank, and do not stretch the line across the whole battlefield. Against Ogres or monsters, brace the anti-large screen and focus damage. Against armoured missile troops, use appropriate armour-piercing tools, cover, and a protected approach. Against undead or regeneration, damage dangerous units and support characters rather than relying on leadership damage alone. After the first collision, pause, face the new threat, and restore formation before pursuing.

## Build to a funded next operation

Use ordinary recurring income as the upkeep floor. Before recruiting or upgrading, account for the proposed army’s actual upkeep, the immediate recruitment/building/cargo cost, and a reserve for replacements and recovery. For a short planning window, project cash after the upfront spend plus recurring income after the proposed upkeep, then compare it with the reserve you intend to keep untouched. Use current prices and a reserve appropriate to the active front; do not rely on a universal treasury number, future loot, or an expected caravan sale to make routine upkeep affordable.

Keep these quantities distinct: treasury is cash on hand; gross income is not net income after upkeep; cargo or goods delivered are not sale gold; and a one-off receipt is not recurring income. Harmony is not a currency, and the archived province guidance does not use research or hero recruitment to repair a province’s building-point balance. Route I’s published counters are separate as well. Do not count its victory reward as opening funding. Check the active objective panel rather than inferring credit from a budget estimate.

Fund the next useful recruitment or settlement milestone before several half-funded elite projects. For a rough income-upgrade payback, add construction turns to upgrade cost divided by incremental recurring income after modifiers—not the whole new building total. This simple estimate ignores compounding and military or landmark value; use the live preview and do not treat payback as a complete decision rule. Grow a settlement when its next tier unlocks a useful building and construction is affordable. Specialise settlements by their actual jobs—income, recruitment, recovery, or frontier defence—instead of copying every chain everywhere. Reclassify a frontier when the map changes, and promote a distant foothold only when replacement access or repeated recruitment makes the investment worthwhile.

The archived budget page explicitly avoids a disputed landmark-income assumption: the 8.1 article reports 500 income for House of Secrets while indexed data reports 300. Use the value in your own building preview; neither number is a guaranteed budget input. See the qualified [House of Secrets source note](https://twdb.io/warhammer3/database/buildings/wh3_cp1_cth_special_house_of_secrets/) and the separate Great Embassy record. A reserve is operational flexibility: after a bad battle or lost caravan, reduce or delay optional spending, replenish or replace the force, and restore a defensible home position before the next operation.

## Equipment • simple roles, limited chores

Equip for the next job instead of spreading useful items at random. The following are role recommendations, not claims that any item automatically appears or equips itself.

- Zhao benefits from protection and sustained fighting; add mobility where useful and casting support before redundant damage.
- Keep casting and survival support on the Alchemist. Do not give a backline caster the strongest melee item by default.
- A supporting General or Gate Master needs staying power for its interception and protection role; do not apply Zhao’s personal effects to a generic commander.
- A Caravan Master is far from home during an exposed journey. Useful defensive or leadership gear can be more valuable there than on an idle lord.
- Match banners to the unit they affect: put missile support on a valuable shooter and protection on a key anchor. Read the assigned-character or unit scope.
- Review equipment after a major caravan arrival, new army, or before a consequential expedition—not after every small battle. Destination and event rewards are not guaranteed prerequisites for a viable force.

## A light recurring routine

Before an operation, name the objective and destination; check the army, funding, access, garrison, and recruitment gates; decide where the force will recover, who covers home, and what would make you delay or stop. At a useful decision point, finish army movement in a safe stance and check casualties and the actual replenishment forecast. Resolve the current caravan event, inspect arrivals and returning masters, and dispatch only when the cargo, escort, risk, and treasury fit. Queue the next purposeful build; review provincial Harmony after a conquest or a batch of construction, not as a reason to micromanage every settlement each turn. Spend available character points for the character’s role, choose the next useful available research, and equip a new commander. Recheck route progress after the relevant battle, delivery, or construction event. The live VCO panel—not this document or an archive calculator—decides whether it received credit.
