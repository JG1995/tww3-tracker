## Opening

**Win the home war before planning the grand tour**

This is a funded long-range expedition, not a random search and not a requirement to destroy every Skaven faction. Secure a working home economy, then reach the five fixed sites through staging bases and geographic operations.

Zhao’s travelling alchemical workshop: an Alchemist, paired Jade and Jet Lions, chosen artillery and a single Sentinel behind a substantial armoured escort. Every step of that build is a recruitment project, not a turn-one shopping list.

- **Secure Qiang’s province and Hanyu Port first.** Remove the initial enemy and fill the practical early army. The later construct theme should not delay the first war.
- **Budget separately for travel and upkeep.** Caravans can fund the expensive refit, but steady income must keep the company alive between deliveries; leave an emergency reserve and do not count an arrival before it is dispatched. The caravan and alchemy panels on the Faction workshop page cover the operations; the Road research branch keeps the programme profitable, but postpone another active slot when escort troops or a western recovery stop are the urgent constraint.
- **Scout west early.** Use a spare hero or contact through diplomacy to learn who controls the approach. Do not send the only Alchemist away from an army which needs it.
- **Choose the first real neighbour threat.** Finish an enemy obstructing Shang-Yang and a stable launch route. A long list of enemies on the map is not a reason to declare on all of them.

Home can support the army without the next caravan arriving on schedule, and the route to the next base is understood.

## Early → Mid

**Assemble the living forge at Shang-Yang**

Recruit the tools for an expedition while a smaller force can answer threats at home.

- **Develop the expedition’s capital roles.** Build Shang-Yang’s House of Secrets (tier three) and one high-tier assembly hub; income, growth and basic recruitment stay in suitable minor settlements. The route’s Shang-Yang list under Settlements carries the full priority order. Beyond home, pay first for repair, recovery and replacements; a tier-five Great Embassy is optional for this route, not a reason to postpone the expedition.
- **Cap the flagship’s skills in route order.** Iron Scale and a functioning spell toolkit come before optional commercial purchases; take Strange Alchemy on a normal home-development visit rather than forcing an expensive return. The Route II skill block under Armies & skills is the priority queue.
- **Keep the Alchemist with the army.** This hero is part of the expedition’s identity: Piercing imbuement is the default against late armour, and retaining Rust and a reliable damage spell beats buying every active ability before you can use it.
- **Add artillery and guardians gradually.** Aim for crossbows, a proper screen and useful cannons first; introduce the paired lions when affordable rather than replacing all infantry with monsters. The Jinshen Company grows into the Alchemical Survey as recruitment and budget allow.
- **Create a home-response force.** A General with modest troops can support a garrison — the 12-slot Western provincial guard under Armies is the pattern. Specify which approach it protects before Zhao leaves, and keep it generic and affordable rather than copying every construct.
- **Set the first target cluster.** Crookback Mountain and Nagashizzar are the eastern fixed objectives. Decide the path between them from the current owner, enemy forces and available recovery points — not an imagined straight line through mountains.

The flagship can fight a siege and a field battle, home has a response plan, and the first two objectives have been scouted.

## Mid → Late

**Complete the eastern operation and establish a western bridge**

Build an actual supply chain before stretching to the Empire.

- **Resolve Crookback Mountain.** Raze or conquer as the active objective requires; check the recorded result before leaving. A sack alone is not assumed to qualify.
- **Prepare the Nagashizzar approach.** Take the access, staging and replenishment route that exists in this campaign. Mountains, corruption and additional hostile stacks can make the apparent shortcut the slow option.
- **Keep only useful intermediate land.** A mine, pass or recovery point can justify permanent investment. A remote settlement taken only for the checklist usually does not need a full elite-recruitment complex.
- **Open the next theatre diplomatically.** Identify a safe route toward Hell Pit and Mordheim, or a southern approach toward Skavenblight when that is more practical. Avoid breaking useful treaties just to shorten the map line.

Compass direction and provincial research support the western bridge — the common research queue for Route II is already selected (open Research on the reference panels) — but Zhao has no teleport mechanic. The road to each site is real travel; the repair-and-recovery stop is the plan, not a teleport.

**The five fixed sites**

Each record names the theatre and what the live campaign must confirm before the operation. The eastern pair comes first; the western sites are separate operations.

- **Crookback Mountain — eastern objective.** Scout the approach and current owner; occupy or raze, then verify the live credit.
- **Nagashizzar — eastern / southern operation.** Use a prepared staging route through the actual campaign geography; do not assume a straight march through mountains.
- **Hell Pit — northern Old World.** Bring monster answers and enough remaining strength for a relief army after the siege.
- **Mordheim — Empire / diplomatic check.** Check current ownership. Explore legal acquisition before starting an avoidable war with an Order partner.
- **Skavenblight — western culmination.** Arrive with the repaired expedition and a clear retreat/recovery route.

**Work the western objectives as separate operations**

Reach the remaining fixed sites with an army which can still sustain losses. Refit toward the Living Forge company, and use the Warpstone Reclamation siege train — or its equivalent — for entrenched objectives; both templates are under Armies.

- **Hell Pit: finish the operation, not just the first battle.** Scout defenders and likely relief armies. Keep enough damage for monsters and enough protection for the backline; do not lose the convoy-like expedition to a second stack.
- **Mordheim: check who owns it.** An Empire owner can turn a military objective into a diplomatic problem. Explore a lawful acquisition if available; do not assume a non-aggression pact or military alliance automatically counts.
- **Skavenblight: arrive prepared.** Treat the last major western objective as a proper siege operation. Bring the siege train or its equivalent, replenish before the final approach and retain the ability to survive a counterattack.
- **Verify earlier site status.** Recheck prior sites after recolonisation or a handover instead of assuming old checks can never change.

All five fixed sites have qualifying live status, or the exact remaining obstruction is known and prepared for.

## Victory push

**Confirm the discovery — and handle its cost**

Win from the actual fixed-site list, not from an invented collection currency.

- **Audit all five names.** Skavenblight, Hell Pit, Mordheim, Nagashizzar and Crookback Mountain. No extra Realms of Chaos city or arbitrary kill quota is added here.
- **Allow the mission to process.** Keep a save before the final interaction and confirm the victory notification. Check site ownership or ruin status if a flag does not register.
- **Record the rewards separately.** Flensing Ruin and the Warpstone Doomrocket arrive after victory; they are not necessary tools for reaching the first target, so do not budget them in the campaign economy before the route completes.
- **Reposition the faction leader thoughtfully.** The added Skaven corruption can make a long stay in a sensitive home province undesirable. Review purification and local Control when continuing into another campaign; the corruption is a trade-off, not an invisible free bonus.
- **Do not widen the route.** No extra ROC targets, no invented warpstone-crafting panel, and no rush into several powerful western factions while the home province is undefended. The qualifying action stays “raze or conquer,” not sack.

VCO has awarded the route, and the reward and corruption changes have been observed rather than assumed.

## Territory policy

Keep enough Cathayan income and defence to support the expedition, then retain productive or strategically useful staging points. Raze a remote objective when that is the sensible legal way to qualify; do not invest heavily in every stop. Recheck a prior site after recolonisation or a handover — credit can depend on the current state.

## Diplomacy

Negotiate practical access through useful neighbours and preserve western Order partners until there is a concrete conflict. Scout current owners before declaring. Explore a settlement purchase or exchange for an Order-held objective where the game permits it; an alliance alone is not assumed to satisfy raze/own.

## Transition → route-1

Bring the expedition’s wealth home without immediately multiplying upkeep. Keep caravans travelling and inspect the completed-run and delivered-goods counters, grow Shang-Yang to tier five and finish the Great Embassy. The added faction-leader corruption from Route II deserves a local administration check before Zhao spends many turns in the commercial core.

## Transition → route-3

Reuse eastern staging bases and the experienced anti-armour company, but switch from passing through territory to holding complete provinces. Bring a second field army to secure borders and finish the three named factions. Distant western conquests are optional funding, not a reason to neglect the four-province goal.
