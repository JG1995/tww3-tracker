## The common opening

- **Keep the company together.** Malakai, Gotrek and Felix are your starting nucleus. Fill affordable gaps with available troops, keep useful starting units, and do not wait for a perfect 20-stack before finishing a favourable opening war.
- **Scout the next approach.** Finish the immediate local threat and watch the Hell Pit approach. Clan Moulder is a high-priority danger when exposed, not permission to ignore an army already marching on Kraka Drak.
- **Build on both maps.** Surface income pays the bills. Ship growth, recovery and movement keep Malakai active. Do not build a complete land recruitment chain simply to duplicate an immediate ship/pool option.
- **Begin a useful Adventure.** Dragonsbane is the archive's normal first choice. Work on the desired Cannon upgrade while taking attainable preparations. Four tasks unlock its finale; the live counters determine credit.
- **Commit to one operational front.** The first route-specific decision is where the second army and first large construction budget go. A second cheap support force may be sensible long before a second premium field army.
- **Make the next battle survivable.** Before ending a turn, check current health, possible enemy reinforcements, movement/stance, and the next replenishment location. A victory that strands a shattered army is not a good operational result.

## Smart Autoresolve • judge casualties, not the label

- **Judge casualties, not the label.** A close victory can be acceptable when the survivors are safe. A decisive victory that deletes your only experienced Hewer or a key character can be strategically worse.
- **Take the forecast seriously.** These are thematic compositions, not an attempt to exploit a hidden score. Do not assume the mod's removal of ranged bias makes Slayers, monsters and airships equally efficient.
- **Preserve a manual-battle fallback.** Keep an escort, tools against large targets, and ranged pressure. Malakai's Spirit summon and character support can make a manual fight very different; their exact autoresolve treatment is unverified.
- **Test the first mission counter.** After a battle used for Cannon kills, an Adventure task or a VCO action, inspect the actual mission. Do not assume the battle result screen proves the scripted counter advanced.
- **Change one thing at a time.** Replace a repeatedly lost specialist with one durable escort or another existing ranged unit, then compare similar enemies. Do not strip every distinctive unit from the army after one bad matchup.

Use the forecast without building around a mystery formula. **Accept** a result when the victory is useful, no unacceptable veteran or character is lost, and the army will be safe until it can replenish. **Reconsider** when an irreplaceable specialist dies, the army is left in range of another hostile stack, or a mission needs a battle action that autoresolve may not credit. **Change the situation** by reinforcing, recovering first, isolating the target, or fighting manually — another expensive barge is not the only solution. The Smart Autoresolve source record marks the evidence boundary: the supplied mod name and description are the only implementation evidence available, no exact weights or matchups were verified, and no benchmark is claimed.

## Manual battle • escort, counterattack, workshop, flight

- **The escort holds.** Keep shielded infantry in compact blocks with gaps for direct-fire guns. Do not order a general advance merely because the enemy has reached the front.
- **Slayers counterattack.** Keep Slayers and Doomseekers out of incoming missile fire, then send them into an engaged target. Pirates work close to the screen rather than as an isolated skirmish line.
- **The workshop chooses targets.** Cannons handle large threats and useful long-range targets. Short-range Hewers need a protected lane. Stop or retarget weapons firing into your own fighting troops.
- **The air group stays one job.** Use the gyro/barge group together near supporting ground fire. Clear enemy anti-air and flyers before attempting ambitious raids. No need for a separate mission on every flank.
- **The companions reinforce.** Keep Gotrek and Felix near the important engagement. Healing and protective abilities do not make unsupported characters invulnerable. Pause to choose the response.

Keep a real ground force alive while the aircraft work: the aviation theme is not an instruction to leave only unprotected characters on the ground. Use Guard Mode on the stationary gunline and reserves, and give committed attackers deliberate targets — an idle-unit helper is not a system for preserving your formation.

## Shared spending discipline

- **Keep armies fit to operate.** Pay upkeep, replenish, and protect the recruitment/recovery link before beginning another luxury project.
- **Buy the next relevant unlock.** An actual Cannon supply, a field-army upgrade or a required landmark is more useful than a recruitment building for units you will not use.
- **Build the infrastructure that matches the route.** A conquered province, a two-turn staging stop and an allied city should not get the same development plan.
- **Invest in income with time to repay.** Use recurring income, not assumed future sacks. A building with a long payback may be unnecessary when one final objective remains.

Ask whether the next purchase can survive its upkeep: compare the treasury now, the current net income, the new one-off spending, the additional upkeep, a minimum cash reserve, and a forecast horizon. This is arithmetic, not a live economy model; include supply-line changes or other upkeep effects in the added figure, and never assume future battle loot, trade growth or unreceived victory rewards. For an income-upgrade payback, use the extra income generated by the upgrade, not the whole building's income, and add construction turns; growth, recruitment access and victory landmarks have other value — The Silver Hall's requirement cannot be reduced to its income payback alone.

Before deciding, run the archive's session checklist: the rear has a response plan; useful research and construction are running; the next preparation has a clear action; the important characters are equipped; and the next operation serves the selected route. The guide does not watch the game — use these as reminders rather than compulsory extra campaign objectives.
