## Opening

**Give Nuln breathing room**

Win the starting war without creating three additional fronts.

This is an Empire threat hunt.

The clearest first Elspeth route: a familiar theatre, explicit targets and a thematic finale after victory.

A Morrian escort and a disciplined Nuln gunline: Black Rose knights, Ironsides, artillery and Death magic. The prestigious Amethyst units are a small signature detachment, not a replacement for every soldier.

::claim inferred src=vco-guide
The Graveyard Watch, The Southern Charter and Fozzrik’s Legacy are strategic subtitles. They are not independently verified in-game route titles.
::

- **Finish the immediate opponent.** Defeat the starting hostile army and remove its nearby recruiting base where practical. Consolidate the Nuln/Wissenburg area actually contested in your save; do not attack a neutral Empire town merely to complete a pretty province border.
- **Build a functional first column.** Recruit enough cheap infantry to cover the frontage. Retain useful starting elites. Add three ordinary Handgunners when the Firearms Academy is available, plus Mortars and an Engineer as the economy allows. The Early template is a destination, not a turn-one shopping list.
- **Grow the industrial centre.** Support Nuln’s growth with Fields nearby and establish reliable industry income. Aim for the tier-three Marksman/Foundry combination rather than rushing every settlement equally.
- **Keep the western flank quiet.** Seek sensible Empire trade and non-aggression. Scout the Sylvania-facing approach and the southern passes before the main army leaves. Place the first Garden at a safe eligible location once available.

A secure Nuln core, a near-full healthy army, positive ordinary income, and a known route of recovery.

## Early → Mid

**Break Sylvania’s momentum**

Make the eastern campaign an elimination, not repeated duels with Vlad.

Keep the Black Rose reserve; solve flying and regenerating threats with guns and a disciplined intercept.

Nuln first, then a Sylvania recovery base and a northern departure node.

- **Attack the vulnerable part of the faction.** Use the current army positions to pick an exposed field force or weak recruiting town. Avoid attacking a reinforced capital immediately after a damaging autoresolve. Do not waste several turns chasing an immortal character while intact cities recruit new troops.
- **Take a base you can recover in.** Keep a useful Sylvania settlement and repair it before starting the next major siege. Growth/replenishment, Priest access and corruption management matter here more than an instant duplicate of all Nuln’s production.
- **Develop the Countess’s escort.** Build the required Livery and keep two Black Rose units when affordable. Let them pursue exposed ranged units or routed enemies during hand-played battles while progressing Theodore’s active quest.
- **Record what is actually complete.** When the Sylvania objective ticks, turn to the next threat instead of annexing every neighbouring neutral. Count all qualifying battle victories in the live mission; no need to manufacture thirty-five early battles.

Use this in a retained Sylvania base before building an unnecessary second industrial capital.

Keep campaign replenishment on armies making repeated autoresolves; do not confuse it with a healing spell.

First Militia service station → one Temple of Morr supporting the undead campaign → a safe northern/eastern service node.

Sylvania’s live destruction objective is satisfied, or its last holdings are identified and can be finished safely.

## Mid → Late

**Link the relief operations**

Choose the next enemy by danger and travel, not the guide’s printed order.

- **Drycha and the eastern woodlands.** Move towards Gryphon Wood when Wargrove of Woe remains a threat. Keep guns at clear woodland edges, not buried behind tree trunks. Do not expect terror alone to break enemies whose psychology or leadership makes it ineffective.
- **Festus and the northern pocket.** Prepare a supported approach towards Brass Keep when The Fecundites survive. Scout nearby reinforcements and avoid an isolated rush past hostile territory. Carry enough armour-piercing damage and recovery for repeated fights.
- **Khazrak and The Deceivers.** Use reconnaissance and allies to locate surviving forces rather than marching blindly from old starting positions. For The Deceivers, check the exact objective wording in your installed version and investigate remaining cult infrastructure if the faction is still alive after a battlefield defeat.
- **Give the homeland a real second army.** A Master Engineer with the Mid or relief template can defend, finish minor holdings and keep ordinary battles moving while Elspeth takes the difficult engagement. It does not need Theodore or five Amethyst units to be worthwhile.

Two sustainable operational roles, safe recovery points, and a current location or action plan for every unticked target.

Keep her army and the Morrian escort intact while travelling between targets.

A relief army, not a second copy of Elspeth’s unique company.

**Close the ledger deliberately**

Every expensive purchase should remove the actual last obstacle.

Militia service station → one Temple of Morr; affordable infantry/artillery upgrades before widespread Amethyst purchasing.

- **Use the elite detachment, not an entire new army.** Introduce three Amethyst Ironsides when pursuing their Field Test. Add Amethyst rockets when the next target has dense infantry; keep a cannon or equivalent answer to large targets. Preserve the Black Rose pair rather than erasing the theme for another battery.
- **Finish factions, not merely capital sieges.** A surviving minor settlement or hidden remaining force can be the last requirement. Inspect the faction and live objective after each apparent elimination. When an ally has already done the job, redirect immediately.
- **Bring the victory count to thirty-five.** Let relief forces win useful battles against actual remaining enemies. Do not open a destructive new war with an Empire ally simply to generate easy victories. The count is a mission check, not a requirement for thirty-five Elspeth-only victories.
- **Keep Nuln ready for the continuation.** Retain the core army, replenish and leave money for a response. The defence against Tamurkhan is listed by the author as unlocked AFTER Route I completion; do not march east searching for him to unlock it.

Three Amethyst Ironsides → an Amethyst Helstorm; a second rocket later keeps the Black Rose escort intact.

All five target objectives and the 35-battle objective are ticked in game.

## Victory push

**Claim the victory, then defend the city**

Separate campaign success from its thematic epilogue.

- **Verify the VCO completion.** Check the route result and rewards in the actual campaign. The atlas’s checkmarks only record your observation; they cannot trigger the mission.
- **Prepare for the Nuln defence.** If continuing into the newly unlocked Elspeth–Malakai battle, replenish, review magic and bring a balanced answer to monsters and infantry. You do not need to confederate Malakai to satisfy the author’s listed trigger.
- **Finish or choose the next story.** You have completed a route. Continuing south or hunting Fozzrik is optional. Keep experienced troops and the existing school rather than restarting the infrastructure programme.

::claim confirmed src=vco-guide
Completing Route I unlocks the Elspeth/Malakai defence against Tamurkhan. It is not added as a prerequisite here.
::

Do not turn the hunt into a requirement to own the entire Empire. Do not count a wounded Lord as a destroyed faction. Do not make the later defence of Nuln a prerequisite.

Victory confirmed in the game; optional Nuln epilogue completed or deliberately left for later.

## Territory policy

Expand through threatened Empire territory when it improves security or income. Keep a connected core and forward recruiting towns; restore peripheral lands to useful Empire partners when it does not compromise the live objective.

## Diplomacy

Keep Reikland and other viable Empire neighbours useful. A northern ally may finish one of the named targets for you; check the objective before marching there. Do not take on every non-required war for goodwill.

## Transition → route-2

Keep Nuln and the Sylvania recovery network. Move the main column south through a safe access route while the northern relief army becomes the homeland guard. Promote one southern capital to the Charter role once travel and replacements justify it; keep the Black Rose escort and add a Land Ship rather than rebuilding from zero.

## Transition → route-3

Use the secure Empire and experienced gunline as the expedition’s bankroll. Redirect through the Border Princes towards the northern candidates, make useful Dwarf contacts, and add Life support only when the machine contingent actually warrants it. The finished hunt is not a reason to annex every search site.

Best reserved for a developed machine contingent. No Tank or Land Ship yet? Keep the cheaper support you already have.
