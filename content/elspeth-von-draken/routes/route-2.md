## Opening

**Make the departure affordable**

Build a homeland that survives Elspeth’s absence.

This is a territorial coalition.

Choose this for building a prosperous new theatre and making diplomacy part of the victory, not simply a battle checklist.

A well-supported industrial expedition: Ironsides, dependable infantry, a mounted escort and a Land Ship as the later centrepiece. A second Master Engineer leads the regional forces rather than copying Elspeth’s unique entourage.

- **Secure the initial war first.** Win the immediate campaign around Nuln, scout the Sylvania-facing flank and stabilise income. When a nearby enemy can strike the capital at will, remove that threat before claiming the army is ready to depart.
- **Do not complete Route I by habit.** Sylvania may need a preventive war for safety, but hunting all five northern/eastern targets is not required by this route. Seek peace, a credible ally or a defended border where that solves the risk with less diversion.
- **Prepare southbound recruitment.** Reach Handgunners and Mortars first, then tier-three Ironsides/Cannons. A two-knight escort can progress Theodore’s chain while protecting the artillery. Finish the relevant first Field Tests using those actual units.
- **Plan two separate jobs.** The expedition needs a full army. The homeland needs a smaller affordable reserve plus the garrisons and Gardens it can genuinely use. Do not count instant Elspeth travel and its cooldown as a substitute for all defence.

An affordable expedition, a homeland response plan, a gold reserve, and reconnaissance of the southern approaches.

## Early → Mid

**Establish the charter’s first foothold**

Choose a connected southern theatre instead of opening both extremes at once.

A practical line, two mounted protectors and eventually a Land Ship; supporting armies secure ports and connections.

Move construction savings forward. Establish one southern recruitment centre once distance justifies it.

- **Choose the approach from current ownership.** Solland/Fort Soll leads towards the western southern approaches; Averland and Black Fire Pass can support a Border Princes approach. Pick the safer route offered by access and friendly ownership. Do not declare a needless war simply to follow a fixed arrow.
- **Secure a defensible first target sector.** Work on the Eastern/Western Border Princes or the nearer Tilean approach according to the map. Occupy useful connected settlements, suppress the field threat, and stop to recover before extending into the next province.
- **Open the diplomatic ledger early.** Identify which factions hold the required regions. A useful Dwarf or Bretonnian owner may be a prospective qualifying partner instead of a compulsory enemy. Use diplomacy from a position of stability, not a chain of promises to join unrelated wars.
- **Build a service station.** Fields and repaired income come before the complete military complex. If the settlement is Garden-eligible and you will hold it, Militia is a sensible forward support branch.

Keep campaign replenishment on armies making repeated autoresolves; do not confuse it with a healing spell.

Keep a homeland Militia node, then add a legal southern Militia station; College only after the replacement network works.

A stable first southern sector, a place to recover and a clear ownership/treaty plan for its entire province.

## Mid → Late

**Turn a foothold into a functioning region**

Pay for a local replacement pipeline when the journey from Nuln becomes the bottleneck.

- **Take or secure Tilea and the western connection.** Use Miragliano or another suitable safe capital as the regional hub when its ownership and access make sense. A friend holding it need not be betrayed; choose another hub if the coalition is working.
- **Address the Blighted Marshes deliberately.** A Skaven-held objective sector needs reconnaissance and a prepared battle rather than a wounded army walking into an apparent ruin. Bring a reserve and enough anti-infantry firepower without exposing all artillery to fast flankers.
- **Develop the charter capital.** Upgrade the local Marksman chain to tier three and Forges to the level actually needed. Add tier-four production when several armies operate nearby. Keep ports/resources and a useful recovery building; do not make every town a recruiting centre.
- **Hand over the routine work.** A regional Master Engineer relieves the first column, protects supply/recruiting centres and cleans up scattered resistance. Elspeth handles the difficult siege or next main army. Maintain ordinary positive income before a third full army.

The expedition can replace losses locally; the southern budget can support its standing forces without relying on constant sack income.

Prioritise recovery and the rank-12 economic support so the expedition keeps moving.

Regional commander: Cartography and Repair & Maintain fit the long southern theatre.

**Secure the outer provinces**

Finish Estalia, the mountains and Pirate’s Current without losing earlier gains.

Militia Gardens at legal service nodes; broader ordinary-unit upgrades before buying Amethyst for every army.

- **Move west with a deliberate objective.** Secure the Estalian regions required by your live mission through conquest or credited diplomacy. Check who owns the Irrana Mountains; do not assume their original owner or that the province capital covers the full requirement.
- **Prepare the maritime operation.** Pirate’s Current requires a sea or coastal operation when hostile. Stage a healthy army, inspect naval approach and landing, and retain a response to counterattacks. One island conquest does not make the rest of the seven provinces irrelevant.
- **Use the machine theme when it earns its keep.** A Land Ship provides a characterful centrepiece in a protected expedition. Buy it after the line and economy work, not instead of replenishing the army. One or two Amethyst enhancements can mark the veteran expedition without depleting every reserve.
- **Audit all seven provinces together.** A treaty partner losing an earlier town can undo maintained control. Recheck every region, not just the last battle.

Three Amethyst Ironsides → one Amethyst rocket → one Amethyst Land Ship once unlocked. Ordinary regional armies stay useful.

Best reserved for a developed machine contingent. No Tank or Land Ship yet? Keep the cheaper support you already have.

## Victory push

**Make the charter permanent—or go home**

Conclude the control objective without inventing a final ritual.

- **Confirm the route in game.** Inspect VCO’s completion and rewards.
- **Reduce unnecessary war exposure.** Keep a defensible southern realm and trusted allies, or consolidate before travelling elsewhere. A line of expensive exposed ports is not a victory dividend if it constantly demands rescue.
- **Use the established bases for the next route.** The Border Princes service network helps a Fozzrik expedition. The homeland reserve and Garden network make it easier to return north for the Empire threat hunt.

::claim verify-in-campaign src=vco-guide
There is no published extra Elspeth-specific final battle for this route. Do not wait for one to validate a completed objective.
::

Do not confuse capital ownership with full province control. Do not follow the Realms of Chaos Norsca list. Do not raid past the targets and abandon them before the route is complete.

Route II victory confirmed; the next theatre, or the campaign’s end, is a deliberate choice.

## Territory policy

Keep worthwhile southern provinces and one regional production hub. Use trusted qualifying partners for peripheral territory when possible; no artificial province limit. Islands, ports and passes deserve a separate defence decision.

Treat ports, islands and the approaches to your regional hub as separate response problems; seven required provinces must stay credited.

## Diplomacy

Prefer a coalition with useful Dwarf, Bretonnian and Empire neighbours where the live mission credits them.

::claim verify-in-campaign src=vco-guide
Do not assume non-aggression or a trade agreement counts as control. Check the actual treaty wording and objective after any upgrade or transfer.
::

## Transition → route-1

Leave a regional commander protecting the Charter’s valuable ports and recruiting hub. Return Elspeth to a safe homeland Garden, inspect which of the five targets are still alive, and bring the escort/artillery mix appropriate to that threat. Your earlier victories may already help the 35-battle requirement; trust the live count.

## Transition → route-3

The Border Princes and southern production already supply the expedition. Preserve valuable alliances, move the main column east into the nearest candidates and use the existing hub for replacements. Diplomatic provincial control and a successful search interaction are not automatically the same event.
