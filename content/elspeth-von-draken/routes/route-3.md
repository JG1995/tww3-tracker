## Opening

**Prepare the expedition, not an entire Empire reconquest**

Secure the departure base and the research company.

This is an Arcane expedition.

Choose this for a shorter-purpose expedition with an exploratory theme. The atlas records observations without pretending to know the hidden search implementation.

An arcane-industrial survey column: Ironsides, long rifles, an Engineer and selected war machines. A Jade Wizard supports valuable surviving models in the developed expedition; the early army still needs affordable infantry and an escort.

- **Resolve the starting danger.** Consolidate the Nuln area and deal with whichever nearby enemy can realistically threaten it next. Sylvania is a security question, not a compulsory completed route before your expedition.
- **Build the usable tier-three company.** Aim for the Marksman/Foundry combination and an affordable infantry screen. Retain Black Rose knights while working on Theodore; the later survey template does not require buying tanks at the beginning.
- **Create the research support gradually.** Elspeth already has Death magic. An Amethyst College Garden is a safe magical support investment after immediate recovery needs are met. Additional wizard recruitment matters only when the expedition or another army can afford to use it.
- **Scout and make first contacts.** Send one appropriate agent ahead towards the Border Princes / northern Badlands. Reveal candidates and owners, establish useful Dwarf relations, and plan the actual qualifying interaction rather than just a tour of named places.

A secure homeland, a healthy developed army, an affordable reserve, and current ownership information for the first cluster.

## Early → Mid

**Open the northern search corridor**

Start close enough that one setback does not require sailing around the world.

A sustainable mixed column with scouting, anti-large fire and a limited machine contingent; Life support only once useful.

One expedition recovery foothold before an expensive second capital. Promote it only if the theatre persists.

- **Choose the safer gateway.** Use the Border Princes / Black Fire Pass approach supported by access and friendly neighbours. Do not march through an ally’s enemy provinces in forced march assuming an alliance makes the road safe.
- **Check the northern candidates first.** Survey ownership around Doz Karaz, Barag Dawazbag and Varenka Hills where they suit the live map. Use the next nearest candidates rather than insisting this is a verified shortest path. The names are candidates, not a fixed three-site unlock.
- **Test the mission interaction early.** After a conquest or newly qualifying alliance, inspect the VCO objective and any event. Record the observed result. If nothing updates, resolve the instruction before investing ten more turns in an assumed method.
- **Keep one service foothold.** Select a settlement that supports recovery and replacement access. Repair, add Fields and working income. A friendly Dwarf-held objective may help the search without allowing a Garden there.

Keep campaign replenishment on armies making repeated autoresolves; do not confuse it with a healing spell.

Secure recovery first; a safe College supports the research theme, while a legal forward Militia node sustains the distant column.

One observed qualifying search interaction and a supported path to the next candidate group.

## Mid → Late

**Keep the field laboratory moving**

Combine cooperation with a selective campaign against hostile candidate owners.

- **Work one contiguous cluster at a time.** Choose the current grouping that minimises hostile crossings: the Ekrund/Dragonhorn area, central Iron Rock/Valaya/Crooked Fang area, or another nearby set in your save. Do not zigzag from the top to the bottom of the entire candidate list.
- **Give diplomacy a practical job.** A treaty is useful when it credits held candidate regions and reduces a front. Read the mission’s qualifying relationship; trade alone is not enough. Do not attack a friendly hold to repeat work already accepted by the game.
- **Use the next real unit milestone.** Tier-four Nuln production permits rockets and a Land Ship. A cannon, a protected rifle section and a line of infantry remain valuable against large targets. Add a Jade Wizard when expensive single entities justify manual healing; keep army recovery supported by skills and bases.
- **Do not promote every stop to a capital.** Replenish at the foothold, then resume operations. If distance becomes persistent and several armies arrive, develop a true regional hub. Until then, global recruitment or transferred replacements can cost less than a redundant industrial complex.

The next search interaction is reachable with a healthy army, a known return route and enough money to replace ordinary losses.

Bring Blessed by Shyish ahead of personal luxury damage; keep travel and recovery useful.

The survey support commander: sustain machines, scout effectively and bring a Wizard.

**Follow the evidence to the final search result**

Stop searching when the mission says the search is finished.

Safe College node + forward Militia support; normal machine upgrades and a small Amethyst research detachment.

- **Push farther only when needed.** When the mission remains active, continue through reachable southern or eastern candidates such as the Morgheim/Deff Gorge area or the coastal southern group. Use current map adjacency and ownership to choose the sequence.
- **Protect the signature machines.** Treat the Land Ship or Tank as supported weapons, not a lone battering ram. Avoid costly autoresolve losses to the Amethyst detachment. Recover one turn if it prevents several turns of replacement logistics.
- **React to discovery, not a fabricated percentage.** The public guide lists twenty possible locations but no numeric quota or complete search script. This atlas shows visited/planned observations, not a claimed x/20 victory bar. Record the fortress-found event and any subsequent live mission instructions.
- **Do only the actual finishing task.** If your installed version presents a dilemma, building or battle after discovery, complete that instruction. None is invented as a universal prerequisite here. If the route completes directly, you are finished.

## Victory push

**Bring the research home**

Preserve the expedition and choose what its footholds become.

- **Verify the reward.** Confirm Route III completion and inspect the custom follower in your campaign. Research and loot benefits do not establish that you can recruit, fly or teleport a fortress unit.
- **Consolidate intelligently.** Keep productive territory, hand over suitable peripheral gains, and reuse the surviving troops. Check the final mission status before disposing of a newly found location.
- **Turn the expedition into a new story.** Expand the Border Princes foothold into Route II’s maintained provincial control, or send Elspeth back through a safe Garden to support the homeland threat hunt. Supporting armies still need their own route home.

Route III victory confirmed; surviving armies and footholds have a deliberate next assignment.

## Territory policy

Keep a secure homeland and selected connected footholds. Expand widely when resistance or income requires it, but do not assume all twenty candidates must be owned. Candidate credit, transit rights and Garden eligibility are separate tests.

Do not invent a search quota or guaranteed location. Do not count an agent walking into a region as the required conquest/diplomacy interaction. Do not treat the reward as a playable flying fortress.

A search interaction is not proof the region is safe to transfer. Recheck the mission, then spend only for your next operation.

## Transition → route-1

Do not dismantle the expedition before a safe return is arranged. Leave a cheap holding force or transfer expendable footholds, then return Elspeth to the homeland Garden network. Identify live targets, restore a two-knight escort if desired, and use the army’s existing guns rather than rebuying a new theme.

## Transition → route-2

Promote the best foothold to a permanent Charter hub and shift from selective searching to maintained province control. A visited candidate does not mean its entire province is secured. Build the additional line/replacement capacity and coastal defence needed to hold seven provinces together.
