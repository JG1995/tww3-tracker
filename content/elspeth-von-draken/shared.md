Elspeth von Draken, the Grey Lady of Nuln and the Empire's deadliest sorceress, commands the black-powder armies and Amethyst battle-magic that hold the realm against its many predators.

## The common foundation

Independent routes still share a sensible beginning: keep Nuln safe and make a working gunpowder army.

**Win the initial local war.** Do not open several fresh fronts. Clear the immediate hostile army and nearby base according to the current map. Keep useful starting elites and assets.

**Fund recovery and tier-three recruitment.** Fields + industry; one advanced centre. Unlock the practical gunline in Nuln; use a nearby support town for a profession or cavalry chain.

**Keep three Handgunners until credited.** Field Testing is specific. A newer or more expensive unit does not automatically satisfy a named unit-type objective.

**Recruit a second army for a real job.** Use the budget, not an arbitrary turn number. An independent expedition needs a home response; a local hunt can let a second army handle minor threats. Project ordinary income after upkeep and leave a recovery reserve.

**Choose the route’s geography.** The northern hunt is not compulsory for all routes. Sylvania can still be a security problem. Solve that problem deliberately rather than being dragged into all of Route I by default.

**Keep one next objective visible.** Use the Field notes page. Record the next destination and its prerequisite. The atlas does not read your game or auto-advance the campaign.

## Autoresolve the operation, not just the battle

The recommendations are sensible mixed forces, not claimed benchmarks of the mod.

**Inspect survivors, not only “victory”.** An elite wipe can be strategically expensive. Judge projected losses to Amethyst units, characters and the recovery of the line. A routine win followed by an immediate second hostile attack can be a poor trade.

**Maintain a healthy line and damage mix.** Infantry, gunfire and a large-target answer. Do not import vanilla ranged-bias doomstacks as an assumed optimum for a matchup-aware mod.

**Recover before a difficult follow-up.** One rest turn may save several recruiting turns. Use the Priest, appropriate skills, Fields and Garden support where they apply. Skills or spells that heal in battle are not automatically campaign replenishment.

**Test mission credit once.** Field tests and Theodore use named events. Observe the counters before and after a representative autoresolve. A named ability-use objective may still require a manual battle.

**Fight the exception manually.** A valuable unit or a special quest can justify it. The army remains designed to work on the battlefield, not only as a number in the preview. Slow down or pause to protect the formation.

**When increasing difficulty later.** Change support before erasing the theme. Keep the Black Rose pair, the signature machine or the Amethyst core; add better scouting, reinforcement and a healthier reserve rather than automatically converting to one-unit spam.

**Scope of testing.** The website is tested as a website. Neither a complete in-game campaign nor your exact mod stack has been simulated here. There are no invented autoresolve success percentages in this atlas.

## Build to a next operation

A strong army and a modest reserve beat four half-funded projects.

**Use ordinary income as the floor.** Exclude uncertain future sacking. Before recruiting another army, subtract its actual upkeep from the projected income. Loot can accelerate development but should not be the only thing keeping ordinary garrisons paid.

**Keep a replacement and travel reserve.** Gold is also operational flexibility. Reserve enough for a meaningful replacement batch and a Garden move when needed. Use your actual prices rather than a hard-coded universal treasury minimum.

**Fund the next recruitment milestone.** Tier three, then purposeful tier four. Nuln’s advanced building queue is valuable only when you can afford to recruit the troops it unlocks.

**Grow provinces you intend to develop.** Do not spam empty tier upgrades. Spending on growth makes most sense when the next tier opens a desired building and its construction will be affordable.

**Specialise by location’s job.** Income, hub, recovery, frontier or survey. Do not build identical three-slot recipes around a port, valuable resource and exposed border.

**Promote a foothold only when needed.** Route II usually needs it sooner. Permanent local production earns its cost when distance and repeated recruitment demand become the bottleneck. Route III may finish without it.

## Give equipment a job

A small assignment routine is enough; no need to rearrange every item every turn.

**Protect Elspeth and the front characters.** Useful survival is not wasted damage. Prefer appropriate resistances, armour and health for the character exposed to the next threat; read the item’s actual scope.

**Give gun-support items to an Engineer.** Match an aura to the units near it. The school’s Engineer equipment should support a real gun/artillery section. An army aura is not automatically factionwide.

**Put casting support on the active Wizard.** One principal casting job first. Keep magical reserves/cost tools with a caster who uses them. Avoid spreading every useful item across idle agents.

**Keep cavalry and machine roles distinct.** Theodore is not the battery Engineer. Frontline survival and shooting support serve different recipients. Equip by role rather than giving every rare item to the legendary Lord.

**Review before an expedition or quest.** Not after every insignificant skirmish. This is a convenient cadence for item fusion, unwanted followers and fresh rewards without endless administrative clicks.

**Never assume automatic assignment.** The atlas does not operate the game. Its notes can record where key items went. Any equipment-management mod is a separate installation with its own behaviour.
